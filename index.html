My Process

Content Publishing Workflow

When an audience depends on accurate information, publishing is a process. At Elephas, every claim is backed by data and approved before launch. The same discipline applies anywhere trust is the product, from healthcare to public safety.

  1. Draft

    Copy aligned to positioning and the content roadmap.

  2. SME Review

    Scientists verify data, methods, and terminology.

  3. Regulatory Review

    Claims checked against published evidence.

  4. Build

    Pages, forms, and emails built with tracking.

  5. QA and Publish

    Links, mobile layout, and metadata tested.

  6. Track

    Results reported in HubSpot and Google Analytics.

Regulatory Review Checklist

  • Performance claims tie back to published, cited data
  • Research Use Only and clinical messaging stay separate
  • Trademarks are used consistently
  • Forms include privacy and marketing consent language
  • Conference posters are approved before posting
  • Outdated positioning is removed sitewide

Relevance Beyond Biotech: brands serving hospitals, government agencies, and first responders carry the same stakes. Accuracy and compliance are what protect their trust.

Brand Guidelines in Practice

The Elephas brand guide defines the logos, colors, type, and naming rules. I apply them as reusable standards across every HubSpot page, email, and post.

Logo System

  • Full color logo on white or light backgrounds, reverse logo on dark backgrounds
  • Product logos like elive™ lead when the product is the subject, with a smaller corporate logo as the sign off
  • Elephas Laboratories logo for LDT content, kept separate from RUO platform claims

Color Palette

  • Purple and Warm Gray are the primary colors, anchored by navy and slate
  • Every color is used at 100, 75, and 15 percent tints
  • The green accent is used sparingly and only when necessary
  • A light Warm Gray gradient is the standard background

Typography

  • Montserrat Light for headlines
  • Montserrat Bold for subheads and call outs
  • Montserrat Regular for body copy, with Corbel as the alternate font

Naming and Trademarks

elive™ Lowercase, with the trademark on the first mention on each page

elive Edge Component names are always initial capped

elive vs elive Test elive is the RUO platform, elive Test is the LDT clinical service

  • I check every page, email, and post against these rules before it goes to regulatory review
  • Keeping RUO and LDT language separate protects every claim we publish

Logo Integrity

  • Minimum width of 100 pixels in digital and 1 inch in print
  • Clear space around the logo on every side
  • Never rotate, stretch, recolor, rearrange, or retype the logo, or place it on busy backgrounds

Web Analytics

Website and Conversion Tracking

What I Analyze

  • Monitor sessions, new contacts, and landing page performance in HubSpot
  • Find where visitors drop off before converting

Key Finding

Landing page views rose 59% in 30 days, but form submissions stayed at zero.

Takeaway: traffic wasn't the problem, conversion was. Next step is a stronger CTA and shorter form.

Channel Analytics

Traffic Sources and Engagement Quality

What I Analyze

  • Compare visits, bounce rate, and time on site by source
  • Balance channels for reach and quality

Key Finding

Organic search bounced far less than direct traffic (63% vs 82%), and social visitors were the most engaged.

Takeaway: SEO for volume, social for engagement.

Paid Media Analytics

Display Performance and Benchmarks

What I Analyze

  • Compare CTR by partner and targeting against benchmarks

Key Finding
